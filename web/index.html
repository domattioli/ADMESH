ADMESH mesh generator

ADMESH builds triangular meshes for two-dimensional shallow-water models. It sizes the triangles from the shape of your domain, then moves the points until the triangles are even, using the DistMesh method with Delaunay triangulation.

The real ADMESH Python package runs in this page. Your files stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

Mesh a domain

A .json or .toml polygon file, or a fort.14 mesh.

Starting

Choose a domain file and press Mesh. The mesh and its checks appear here.
